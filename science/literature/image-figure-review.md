# Microscopy figure clarity audit

BioSkillsHub literature adaptation; not authored or endorsed by the paper's authors. Expert review pending; not experimentally validated.

## Use cases
Review image-based manuscript figures before submission.

## Inputs
Original images, proposed panels, pixel calibration, channel identities and caption; a calibrated image viewer and figure editor.

## Outputs
Annotated revision list and a caption checklist.

## Procedure
1. Confirm what organism, tissue and structure each panel depicts.
2. Check scale information against calibration; include readable scale bars and units.
3. Mark inset locations and explain enlarged views.
4. Explain channels, colours, arrows and abbreviations in the legend.
5. Inspect at final publication size and with a colour-vision accessibility check; avoid conveying meaning through colour alone.

## Expert decisions
Choose magnification and annotations that expose the relevant observation without hiding context.

## Limitations
Visual clarity does not validate sampling, quantification or biological claims. Stop if calibration or image provenance is missing.

## Examples
For a two-channel panel, supply separate channel identities, an inset boundary and a calibrated scale bar; request missing information instead of guessing.
